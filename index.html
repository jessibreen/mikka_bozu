    How it works

    Mikka bōzu (三日坊主) means "three-day monk": someone who gives up after three days. So only commit to three.

    Each habit runs in blocks of three days. Check it off three days in a row and you earn a block. Then the next three days begin.

    Miss a day and that block quietly disappears. Nothing is counted against you. A fresh Day 1 is waiting whenever you come back.

    You can only check off today, and you can undo it until midnight.
